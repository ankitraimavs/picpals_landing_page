Data Deletion Request

Introduction

At WeClick, we value your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. Since WeClick uses Google Sign-In only, we do not store separate passwords. This page explains how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send a request using the form below, or email us directly at feedback@yonderwonder.ai.
  2. Provide the Google account email you used to sign in to WeClick.
  3. Our team will verify your request.
  4. We will delete your account and associated data within 14 working days of verification.

Submit a Request

Response Timeline

We respond to all verified data deletion requests within a maximum of 14 working days.
