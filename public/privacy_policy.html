Quick summary: See the detailed sections below for full terms. In short — we collect account details (email/phone), prompts you provide, and media you upload. 1) Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

Email address, mobile number for OTP verification (via a secure SMS provider), Google account ID if you choose Google sign-in, Apple account ID if you choose Apple sign-in, display name, and authentication logs.

1.2 Media Content

Media (e.g., photos, videos) and prompts you upload to generate group images or other outputs. We may read hidden EXIF metadata only for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or to perform necessary technical functions such as correctly rotating or orienting the image. We do not otherwise use EXIF data for unrelated purposes.

1.3 Device Data

Device type, operating system version, language, and app version. We do not collect analytics, usage, or diagnostic data.

1.4 Payments (if applicable)

Payments for credits or other in-app purchases are processed securely through official platform providers (e.g., Google Play, Apple App Store) or other PCI-DSS compliant processors. We do not store or have access to full credit/debit card numbers. All purchases are final and non-refundable except where required by law or platform policy. Please review purchase details before confirming, as transactions cannot be reversed once processed. See our Terms of Service for full payment and refund terms.

1.5 Future Features & Additional Data

We may introduce new features in future that collect additional types of data. If so, we will update this Policy and, where required by law, seek your consent before collecting such data.

2) Media Processing & Retention

  • Uploaded media is processed on secure systems and retained for up to 60 days so you can re-download, edit, and recover outputs.
  • If you delete your account, your uploaded media and personal account information are deleted immediately and permanently. This deletion is final and cannot be reversed.
  • You may also request earlier deletion of your media at feedback@yonderwonder.ai. Where technically feasible, we will honor early deletion requests.

Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction/objection, and to withdraw consent. The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If we learn we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will delete it.
